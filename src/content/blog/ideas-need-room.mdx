An idea often disappears because we ask it to arrive complete. A public notebook gives early thoughts somewhere to live while they are still changing shape.

## Notes are allowed to be provisional

The value of a note is not that it has the last word. It captures a useful model at a particular moment, with enough context for a future version of me to continue.

## Writing reveals the missing edges

Explaining an idea forces it to touch reality. Vague confidence turns into specific questions, and those questions become the next thing to learn.
