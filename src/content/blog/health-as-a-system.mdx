Good systems reduce the number of decisions required on an ordinary day. Health can work the same way: simple defaults, visible feedback, and enough flexibility to survive real life.

## Start with the boring layer

Sleep, water, regular meals, sunlight, and movement are not novel. That is exactly why they are useful. They are infrastructure, not an optimization trick.

## Track signals, not identity

A missed workout is a data point. It is not evidence about who you are. The useful question is whether the system made the next good action easy enough.

## Leave room

A sustainable routine has slack. Recovery and unstructured time are part of the plan rather than failures to execute it.
